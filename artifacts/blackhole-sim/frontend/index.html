Scientific Visualization Guide

This simulation visualizes the extreme gravitational lensing effects of a Schwarzschild/Kerr black hole surrounded by a thick, glowing accretion disk.

Accretion Disk Lensing

Due to intense gravitational bending, light rays from the portion of the disk behind the black hole are bent around it, making the rear top portion appear above the event horizon, and the rear bottom portion appear below the horizon.

Interaction

  • Left-Click & Drag Background: Orbit camera around the black hole.
  • Scroll Wheel: Zoom camera in and out.
  • Left-Click & Drag Emitter: Move the interactive light source.
  • Double-Click: Reposition the light source on the equatorial orbit.
  • R Key: Reset camera view.

HUD Controls

Toggle Settings, Camera parameters, and Ray Simulation modes using the right-side vertical toolbar.
